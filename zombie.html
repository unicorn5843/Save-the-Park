How to Survive

The Swarm

Zombies stream in from the map edges and head straight for your officers or the station. They never attack buildings.

Supply crates drop onto the road network. Walk any officer over one to bank its contents.

Test-Subject Grabbers seize live zombies and drag them back to the station. Every delivery pays out supplies based on the specimen's value.

Acid officers spray a cone of dissolving acid that eats through a crowd. The acid van drives the cordon and drenches anything nearby. K9 hunts runners, the ambulance patches up wounded syndicates, and the repair crew tidies up damaged streets.

Survive the wave, harvest the swarm, hold the line.
