How to Survive

The Swarm

Zombies stream in from the map edges and head for your officers or the station.

Supply crates drop onto the road network. Walk any officer over one to bank its contents.

Test-Subject Grabbers seize live zombies and drag them back to the station for supplies.

Acid officers spray a dissolving cone that eats through a crowd. The acid van drives the cordon and drenches anything nearby. K9 hunts runners, the ambulance patches up wounded syndicates, and the repair crew tidies up damaged streets.

Use formations, junction posts, and ALL OUT to hold the line.
